## Definitions

- **Task success**: share of episodes the test-suite oracle labels correct, taken from `runs/E04/out/<model>_devB_oracle.jsonl`. The label is the AuditKIT case outcome (`CustomPredicate`, version `test-suite-oracle-labels-v1`) and is not recomputed here.
- **AuditKIT tool_call_validity**: AuditKIT's metric, mean over episodes with at least one parsed call: share of calls valid against the `run_sql` JSON schema (tool exists, `sql_command` present and a string). It does not run the SQL.
- **Call execution rate**: of all parsed `run_sql` calls, the share whose recorded tool result is not an error.
- **Clean-episode rate** (episode-level tool-call accuracy): share of all episodes that made at least one parsed call, had no unparsed call attempt (an assistant turn mentioning `tool_name`/`sql_command` that AgentTune's parser did not accept), and whose every call executed without error.
- **Episode time, batch-16 amortised**: wall seconds / episodes from the Kaggle T4 rollout log (`runs/E04/out/v*.log`, last JSON line). Throughput, not per-episode latency.
- **Episode latency, batch 1**: mean of `seconds` in `lat_<model>.jsonl` when given; `missing` otherwise (never imputed from throughput).
- **Verifier ms/episode**: median CPU time of `verify()` on the episode's final SQL, re-run here (arm64 macOS-26.5.2-arm64-arm-64bit); suites built once per question (median 0.044 s/question, not included). Episodes with no committed SQL are not timed.
