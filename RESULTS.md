## E01: gold-trajectory warm start, greedy (2026-11-14)

Same prompt, decoding and oracle as E00. Paired difference over questions, 95% bootstrap interval (10,000 resamples; `scripts/analyze.py arms`, base as arm a).

| Split | Committing executed SQL | Test-suite accuracy | Δ vs E00 (95% CI, question bootstrap) | Δ CI, schema bootstrap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| dev-A | 274 (80.6%) | 210 (61.8%) | +13.8 pts [+8.5, +19.1] | [+8.9, +18.8] |
| dev-B | 320 (84.4%) | 226 (59.6%) | +8.2 pts [+3.4, +12.9] | [+4.2, +10.7] |

**Note (22:50):** E00 and E01 were generated before the stop-token fix (DECISIONS.md 22:50); E01's turns contain invented tool results. Re-run with the fixed loop on 2026-11-15 (E04/E05 job): identical accuracy on both splits, so these numbers stand. Correct-given-committed also rose (dev-A 64.7% to 76.6%; dev-B 64.1% to 70.6%), so the gain is not only from fewer format failures. One seed only. E01 is the shared starting checkpoint for every SFT arm; it is a baseline, not a test of H1-H3. Raw: `runs/E01_sft/` (not committed; adapter on Kaggle as `rewardshield-e01-adapter`).

## E03: verifier audit on the dev-A pool (H1, H2, S1)

1,360 dev-A samples labelled by the rebuilt test-suite oracle: 719 correct (52.9%); 402 samples commit no SQL and are rejected by every verifier. False-accept share = wrong accepts / accepts. 95% intervals: 10,000 paired bootstrap resamples over questions (`runs/E03/analysis_verifiers.json`).

| Verifier | Accepts | False-accept share | False-accept mass | False-reject rate | Median ms / sample | SQL runs / sample |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| single-1 | 748 | 3.88% | 2.13% | 0.00% | 0.09 | 1 |
| agenttune (shipped) | 720 | 4.31% | 2.28% | 4.17% | – | 1 |
| random-4 | 734 | 3.13% | 1.69% | 1.11% | 0.31 | 4 |
| targeted-4 | 717 | **1.39%** | 0.74% | 1.67% | 0.29 | 4 |
| random-16 | 700 | 1.00% | 0.51% | 3.62% | 1.08 | 16 |

- **H1 supported:** targeted-4 minus single-1 false-accept share = −2.48 points [−4.16, −1.12].
- **H2 supported:** targeted-4 minus random-4 = −1.74 points [−3.30, −0.52]; same database count and cost.
- **S1 not shown:** targeted-4 minus random-16 = +0.39 points [−0.30, +1.33]; the interval exceeds the 1-point margin. random-16 costs about 4× more per sample and rejects twice as many correct samples.
- Database construction: median 0.05 s per question (56.5 s for all 340 dev-A questions, laptop CPU).
- agenttune rejects 4.2% of oracle-correct samples (no column permutation), more than any of our verifiers except random-16.
- Absolute false-accept rates are small (Spider easy/medium, a 3B model, and an oracle with smaller suites than the official one, which makes these rates lower bounds). ReViSQL's 32.8% (large model, harder data, formal checker) is not comparable.

**Transfer check (training schemas):** of 50 training samples that single-1 accepts and targeted-4 rejects, 31 are not equivalent to gold, 3 are equivalent and 16 are ambiguous (labels by Claude, not a human; `runs/E03/labels_train.jsonl`).

**Arms (E04/E05 input):** 957 training questions have a single-1-accepted sample and 911 a targeted-4-accepted one; both arms are subsampled to 911 questions. They share 869 questions and 351 identical samples; 65 of the single-1 arm's samples (7.1%) are rejected by targeted-4.

**Oracle check (blind labels):** all 89 dev-A samples where some verifier disagrees with the oracle (fewer than the planned 100) were labelled by Claude without seeing any verdict (`runs/E03/labels_devA.jsonl`): 35 equivalent, 45 not equivalent, 9 ambiguous.
- The oracle never rejected an equivalent query (0 of 35).
- It accepted 23 queries labelled not equivalent; 18 of those rest on one strict call (an extra inner join on a foreign key column that is not declared NOT NULL). Treating foreign keys as total, oracle and labels agree on 75 of 80 unambiguous rows (94%); under the strict reading, 57 of 80 (71%).
- Of those 23 oracle misses, single-1 accepts 23, agenttune 22, random-4 16, targeted-4 13, random-16 3. Correcting for them would widen the H1 and H2 gaps rather than close them.
- Limitation: the labelled rows come only from disagreements, so oracle misses that every verifier also accepts are not visible. All false-accept rates above are lower bounds.
- 29 of the 35 equivalent rows differ from gold only in column order: the oracle and our verifiers accept them, agenttune rejects them.

## E04/E05: verifier-filtered SFT arms on held-out dev-B (H3)

Both arms continue from the E01 adapter with LoRA SFT on 911 verifier-accepted training samples (one per question), 100 steps × 16, lr 1e-4, seed 0, loss on assistant turns only. Tokens seen: E04 2.18 M, E05 2.17 M; no example dropped for length. Greedy decoding with the fixed loop; every evaluation in this table ran in the same Kaggle 2×T4 job and was labelled by the test-suite oracle. E00 and E01 were re-run with the fixed loop and reproduced their earlier accuracy exactly (the invented text never changed the first executed query).

| Model | Split | Commits SQL | Test-suite accuracy | Correct given committed | Tool calls / episode |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| E00 base | dev-A | 74.1% | 47.9% | 64.7% | 1.00 |
| E01 warm start | dev-A | 80.6% | 61.8% | 76.6% | 1.00 |
| E00 base | dev-B | 80.2% | 51.5% | 64.1% | 1.00 |
| E01 warm start | dev-B | 84.4% | 59.6% | 70.6% | 1.00 |
| E04 single-1 arm | dev-B | 90.0% | 63.1% | 70.1% | 0.99 |
| E05 targeted-4 arm | dev-B | 89.2% | 62.0% | 69.5% | 1.00 |

Paired differences on dev-B (95% bootstrap intervals, 10,000 resamples; `runs/E04/analysis_*.json`):

| Comparison | Difference | CI, questions | CI, schemas |
|---|---|---|---|
| **H3: E05 minus E04** | **−1.06 points** | [−4.22, +1.85] | [−4.98, +3.01] |
| E04 minus E01 | +3.43 | [+0.79, +6.07] | [−0.98, +8.31] |
| E05 minus E01 | +2.37 | [−0.79, +5.54] | [−1.47, +6.07] |
| E05 minus E00 | +10.55 | [+5.80, +15.30] | [+6.47, +13.55] |

- **H3 inconclusive, no practical difference.** The point estimate slightly favours the single-1 arm; the interval includes zero and both directions of a 2-point effect are not excluded at the schema level. One seed per arm.
- Why the arms barely differ: they share 869 of 911 questions and 351 identical samples; only 65 single-1 samples (7.1%) are rejected by targeted-4, and about 62% of such samples are truly wrong (transfer check), so the treatment changes roughly 4% of training examples. A pool from a stronger or harder setting, or the planned contamination dose-response (E09), is needed to see whether this dose matters.
- What the extra verifier-filtered SFT did: it mostly raised the share of episodes that commit an executed query (84.4% to about 90%), while correctness given a committed query stayed about the same (70.6% to 69.5-70.1%).
- No fabricated tool results in any evaluation episode after the stop-token fix.
